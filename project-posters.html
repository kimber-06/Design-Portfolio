EMST // VISUAL CULTURE ARCHIVE

Concrete Overprint: NYC Triad

Deconstructing New York's iconic architecture, grid systems, and subterranean noise through an analog-digital zine aesthetic.

Project Scope

3 Screenprints

Deliverables

3 Custom Posters (18"x24")

Core Methods

Xerox Decay, Grid-Breakage

Tools Used

InDesign, Analog Scanner

01 // The Urban Narrative

The challenge was to capture New York City without falling back on cliché skyline photography. I engineered a design system across three distinct physical poster sheets to map out the psychological reality of the city: the rigid, aggressive layout of the Manhattan street grid contrasted against the tactile, decaying wheatpaste poster layers found in transit spaces.

The final suite consists of three cohesive, highly structured 18" x 24" print proofs. Each composition serves as a separate conceptual commentary on NYC's spatial design:

03 // The Mechanical Degradation

To pull the artwork off the screen, I printed the vectors out onto lightweight newsprint stock. I physically scuffed the paper surfaces, applied ink bleeds, and re-scanned them using an industrial high-contrast copier. This chemical degradation step embedded the gritty, tangible soul of NYC directly into the final print deliverables.

"By degrading the typography, the three final prints carry a physical history—repositioning a modern city archive into a raw, tactile zine format."
