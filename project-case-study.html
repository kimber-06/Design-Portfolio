01 / Brand Identity

Sustainable Fashion Startup System

Building an eco-conscious visual identity from the ground up to challenge industry greenwashing.

Role

Lead Graphic Designer

Timeline

4 Weeks (Spring 2026)

Deliverables

Logo System, Brand Guidelines, Digital Assets, Packaging

Tools Used

Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

The Challenge

Most sustainable brands look identical—earthy tones, leaves, and minimal typography. The client needed a distinctive identity that targeted an edgy, Gen-Z demographic while retaining authentic environmental values without looking cliché.

Figure 1.1: Early moodboard establishing color theory and raw typography choices.

The Creative Solution

I built a high-contrast visual system using bold, raw industrial layouts contrasted against deep, organic hues. The centerpiece is a dynamic logo system that physically scales and adapts based on the medium it is placed on, mimicking natural structural growth.

Figure 1.2: Rough digital vector iterations.
Figure 1.3: Finalized vector asset suite.

The Final Impact

The system was implemented across web layouts, recycled cardboard packaging structures, and screen-printed garment tags. The visual framework effectively balanced raw counter-culture design elements with premium, tactile eco-conscious details.

Figure 1.4: Real-world physical packaging and clothing tag mockups.
